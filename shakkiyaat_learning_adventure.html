2. SHAKKIYAAT

23 Types of Doubts

Namaaz mein 23 tarah ke doubts (Shak) ho sakte hain.

8 ❌ Namaaz Baatil
6 ℹ️ Ignore
9 ✅ Valid

❌ 8 Shak — Namaaz Baatil

  • 2 ya 3 Rakat wali Wajib Namaaz mein Rakats ki tadaad par shak.
  • Musafir ki Namaaz mein Rakats ki tadaad par shak.
  • Aur woh Shak jo Namaaz ko baatil karte hain.
🧠 Yaad rakhein: Kuch Shak ki wajah se Namaaz dobara padhni padti hai.

ℹ️ 6 Shak — Ignore kiye ja sakte hain

  • Aise amal ke baare mein shak jiska waqt guzar chuka ho.
  • Salaam complete karne ke baad shak.
  • Namaaz ka waqt guzarne ke baad shak.
  • Mustahab Namaaz mein shak.

✅ 9 Shak — Valid Shak

Ye 4 Rakat wali Namaaz mein Rakats ki tadaad ke doubts hain.

Pehle ruk kar sochiye. Agar kisi taraf zyada probability ho to uske mutabiq amal karein. Agar shak baqi rahe to rules ke mutabiq amal karein.

Example: 2 ya 3 Rakat ka shak → 3 Rakat maan kar Namaaz complete karein + 1 Rakat Namaaz-e-Ehtiyaat.
3. NAMAAZ-E-EHTIYAAT

Precautionary Namaaz

Rakats ki tadaad ke shak ko compensate karne ke liye Namaaz-e-Ehtiyaat padhi jaati hai.

1️⃣ Niyyat
2️⃣ Takbeer
3️⃣ Surah al-Hamd
4️⃣ Ruku
5️⃣ 2 Sajdahs

🕐 1 Rakat Namaaz-e-Ehtiyaat

  • Asal Namaaz ke Salaam ke foran baad Niyyat.
  • Takbeer → Surah al-Hamd → Ruku → 2 Sajdahs.
  • Phir Tashahhud aur Salaam.

🕑 2 Rakat Namaaz-e-Ehtiyaat

  • Pehli Rakat ki tarah doosri Rakat ada karein.
  • Doosri Rakat ke baad Tashahhud aur Salaam.

🚫 Ismein kya nahi hota?

  • Azaan nahi.
  • Iqaamah nahi.
  • Doosri Surah nahi.
  • Qunoot nahi.
  • Niyyat buland awaaz se nahi.
  • Surah al-Hamd ahista padhna chahiye.
Mustahab Ihtiyaat: Bismillah bhi bahut ahista padhi jaye.
4. SAJDAH-E-SAHW

Bhool ka Sajdah

Namaaz mein kuch khaas bhool ya galti hone par Salaam ke baad 2 Sajdah-e-Sahw kiye jaate hain.

📌 Tashahhud bhool jaana → 2 Sajdah-e-Sahw.

🔢 4, 5 ya 6 Rakats ka Shak

4 Rakat Namaaz mein doosri Sajdah ke baad shak ho:

  • 4 ya 5 Rakats?
  • 4 ya 6 Rakats?
➡️ 2 Sajdah-e-Sahw zaroori hai.

⚠️ Ihtiyate Wajib — Sajdah-e-Sahw

  • Namaaz mein koi cheez galti se kam ya zyada ho aur Namaaz valid ho.
  • Namaaz mein galti se baat karna.
  • Galat waqt Salaam keh dena.

Example: Pehli Rakat mein galti se Salaam keh dena.

⭐ Mustahab Ihtiyaat

  • Ek Sajdah bhool jaana.
  • Jahan khade hona tha wahan galti se baith jaana.
  • Jahan baithna tha wahan galti se khade ho jaana.
In cases mein 2 Sajdah-e-Sahw karna Mustahab Ihtiyaat hai.

🧎 Sajdah-e-Sahw ka Tareeqa

1️⃣ Salaam
2️⃣ Niyyat
3️⃣ Sajdah + Zikr
4️⃣ Baithna
5️⃣ Sajdah + Zikr
6️⃣ Tashahhud
7️⃣ Salaam

📝 Sajdah-e-Sahw ka Zikr

بِسْمِ اللهِ وَبِاللهِ، اَلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ

Bismillahi wa billah, assalaamu alaika ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakaatuh.

📌 Sajdah-e-Sahw — Important Notes

  • Qiblah ki taraf rukh hona chahiye.
  • Wuzu mein hona chahiye.
  • Peshani aisi cheez par ho jis par Sajdah jaaiz ho.
  • Saare 7 parts ka zameen par hona zaroori nahi.
  • Ihtiyaat ki bina par 7 parts ko zameen se touch karna chahiye.
