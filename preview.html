3. TASHAHHUD

When do we say Tashahhud?

  • 2nd Rakat of all Waajib and Mustahab Namaaz.
  • 3rd Rakat of Maghrib.
  • 4th Rakat of Zuhr, Asr and Isha.
  • Also necessary in Namaaz-e-Witr.
🧠 Remember: Tashahhud comes after the 2nd Sajdah while sitting.

🔢 Number of Tashahhud

  • 1 Rakat: Witr → 1 Tashahhud
  • 2 Rakat: Subh / Mustahab → 1
  • 3 Rakat: Maghrib → 2 (2nd & 3rd)
  • 4 Rakat: Zuhr / Asr / Isha → 2 (2nd & 4th)
Tashahhud should be recited in correct Arabic, consecutively and in the specified sequence.

🧩 Tashahhud Sequence

1️⃣ Testify Allah
2️⃣ Testify the Holy Prophet ﷺ
3️⃣ Recite Salawaat
⭐ Salawaat is the Waajib part of Tashahhud.
If Tashahhud is forgotten, the Namaaz is not invalid according to the supplied lesson.
4. SALAAM

After the last Tashahhud

  • Say Salaam while sitting in a restful position.
  • The 1st Salaam is recommended before the 2nd or 3rd.
  • One of the 2nd and 3rd Salaams is obligatory.
  • If the 2nd Salaam is recited, the 3rd must also be recited.
  • Salaam should be in proper Arabic.
💡 Salaam is Waajib, but it is not a Rukn. Forgetting Salaam does not invalidate the Namaaz according to the lesson.
5. TARTEEB

What does Tarteeb mean?

Tarteeb = Sequence / correct order.

Every action of Namaaz should be performed in its specified place and time.

6. MUWALAAT

Close Succession

Acts of Namaaz should follow one another normally without a long interruption.

  • Ruku → Sujood → Tashahhud should flow one after another.
  • The recitations should also be connected in a normal manner.
  • If the delay becomes so long that it can no longer be considered Namaaz, the Namaaz is invalid.
7. QUNOOT

Quick Guide

  • Recommended before Ruku of the 2nd Rakat in Waajib and Mustahab Namaaz.
  • Witr: recommended before Ruku even though it is 1 Rakat.
  • Namaaz-e-Ayaat: 5 Qunoots.
  • Eid al-Fitr & Eid al-Azha: 5 in 1st Rakat and 4 in 2nd Rakat.

🤲 Qunoot Hand Position

  • Hands in front of the face.
  • Palms facing the sky.
  • Hands and fingers kept close together.
  • Looking at the palms is Mustahab.
  • Any Zikr is sufficient; even saying “Subhanallah” once is sufficient.
